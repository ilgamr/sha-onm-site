Who we are

A manufacturer first, a supplier second

Most of what we ship is not bought off a shelf. It is produced to a specification — ours or the customer's — at plants we have qualified, on terms we negotiate, under documentation and inspection we control. Production cooperation is the core of the business: we hold the technical package, place the order, supervise the work, and take responsibility for the result.

Alongside our own production programmes we source and supply serial equipment, materials and spare parts, using the same supplier network, engineering capability and export infrastructure. Local market expertise, international trade experience, multilingual communication and modern digital tools reduce procurement risk and shorten lead times for our customers.

Manufacturing & production cooperation

How we build what we sell

We operate as the manufacturer of record for our production programmes. Work is carried out at partner plants selected, audited and contracted by us, under our technical documentation and our quality control — a cooperation model widely used in Chinese heavy industry.

Technical documentation

Drawings, specifications and material requirements are prepared or adapted by our engineers, with conformity to the applicable standards — GB, ASTM, API, DIN, ISO or GOST.

Qualified production base

A network of machining, casting, forging, fabrication and assembly plants, each audited for technical capability, capacity, quality system and export experience.

Tooling & first article

Tooling, prototypes and first-article samples are produced and approved before series manufacturing begins.

In-process supervision

Our own staff attend the plants during production: dimensional checks, welding and heat-treatment control, and stage-by-stage progress reporting.

Material traceability

Mill certificates, chemical and mechanical test reports, heat numbers and material identification retained and supplied with the goods.

Final inspection & testing

Pressure, load, run-in and NDT testing as required, witnessed by us or by a third-party inspection body, before packing and shipment.

For customs and compliance purposes: goods produced under our cooperation programmes are manufactured in the People's Republic of China to our technical documentation, shipped with full manufacturing, quality and origin documentation, and exported under our own contracts.

How we work

From requirement to delivery

01

Understanding the requirement

We review technical specifications, drawings, standards and commercial objectives, and confirm what has to be made, what can be sourced, and what has to be engineered from scratch.

02

Production planning or supplier selection

For manufactured items we prepare the technical package and allocate the work within our production base. For serial goods, manufacturers are identified, verified and evaluated on technical capability, capacity, quality system and export experience.

03

Commercial evaluation

Costs and quotations are collected, compared and analysed to arrive at the most suitable solution — not simply the lowest price.

04

Manufacturing and project execution

We coordinate production, supervision, documentation, inspections and shipment preparation, and report progress at each stage.

05

Export and delivery

We handle export procedures, origin and quality documentation, logistics planning and customs-related matters through to delivery.

Transparency

Whenever project conditions allow, we disclose original manufacturer quotations and supplier identities.

Transparency builds better long-term relationships. Our customers see exactly where value is created — engineering and documentation, plant qualification, production supervision, quality control, export procedures, risk management and project coordination — and make decisions on facts rather than assumptions.
